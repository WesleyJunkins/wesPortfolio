📼 Archive Encryptor

Local tool — run it from your machine. It turns your plaintext video list into the encrypted data.enc blob that ships in the repo. Your real links and password never leave this page.

① Encrypt your list

Save / paste this as data.enc in this archive/ folder, then commit it. Keep your plaintext list in archive/data.source.json (git-ignored) so you can edit it next time.

② Verify (optional)

Paste a blob + the password to confirm it decrypts back to your list.

How adding a video works now (read me)
  1. Upload the home movie to YouTube as Unlisted (or Private — see the README).
  2. Open your local archive/data.source.json and add the link.
  3. Paste its contents into box ① above, enter the archive password, click Encrypt.
  4. Replace archive/data.enc with the output and commit.

The plaintext list is never committed; only the ciphertext is. The links are unreadable to anyone without the password.
